| 284 | The wall fan units are operating normally. | All fan wall units are operating normally. | 改为“所有风墙机组运行正常。” | 数据中心暖通常用 `fan wall`，并补齐 `All` 与中文范围。 |
| 298 | Thank you everyone for today's work. | Thank you, everyone, for today's work. | 保留“感谢大家今天的工作。” | `everyone` 是呼语，规范书写时前后使用逗号。 |
| 300 | Have a safe shift and have a good day. | Have a safe shift and a good day. | 保留“祝大家值班顺利，今天工作愉快。” | 并列祝福中第二个 `have` 可省略，表达更简洁自然。 |
